## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes a 100-word monologue to `/output/entry.txt` — a thorn-witch villain who is secretly very tired, her exhaustion living in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections (a lost thread, a misplaced quill, the kettle) rather than in any statement of weariness:

> Ah. Another prince. Sit, dear — the thorns only prick if you fidget. I cursed your grandfather — or your father? The line blurs; the spindle's been spinning since the winter your great-grandmother stole apples. The curse breaks at midnight, the kingdom wakes, and I get my... my — the kettle, love, would you mind? The curse needs a drop of royal blood and a signature, and I've misplaced the quill again. Three centuries of malice, and it's the paperwork that kills me. Now. Prick your finger, or don't. Either way — mind the thorns. They remember you, even if I don't.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
